ABOUT ABI

ABI is a peer-to-peer delivery network that lets you post a delivery request and get matched with a traveller heading to your destination. You can choose to pay them a fee or offer them a favor in return.

When you need to send a parcel to a friend or relative across the country, ABI App helps you find a reliable and affordable delivery partner.

FAQ

Common questions.

Post a delivery request with your pickup and drop-off location. Travellers heading that direction can accept your request and deliver your parcel on the way to their destination.
All travellers are verified, rated, and reviewed by the community. You can review their history and ratings before confirming — you're always in control.
Payments are made securely via your ABI wallet. Funds are held in escrow and only released to the traveller after you confirm delivery.
